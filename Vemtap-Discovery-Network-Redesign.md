## **Vemtap Discovery Network** Redesign

Redesign the entire **Vemtap Discovery Network** experience across **Business Dashboard**, **Customer App**, and **Super Admin Dashboard**\.

This is a **UI/UX simplification project**, not a feature removal project\.

**Do not remove any existing Discovery Network functionality, attribution tracking, referral tracking, reporting, notification systems, fraud prevention, partnerships, sponsored placements, customer tracking, revenue attribution, automation, or backend logic\.**

All existing functionality must remain intact\.

The goal is to make the experience:

- Extremely simple
- Mobile\-first
- Easy to understand
- Easy to use
- Modern
- Professional
- Clean
- Minimal
- Non\-technical
- Suitable for small business owners

The complexity should remain in the backend\.

The frontend should only expose what users actually need\.

# CORE PRINCIPLE

Businesses do not care about:

- Attribution Engines
- Referral Lifecycles
- Conversion Funnels
- Fraud Detection
- Event Tracking
- Journey Tracking
- Notification Engines

Businesses care about:

1. How many people saw my promotion?
2. How many people visited my business?
3. How much revenue did I generate?

The Discovery Network must feel like:

> "A simple tool that helps businesses get more customers from nearby businesses\."

NOT:

> "A marketing platform full of analytics and technical terminology\."

Replace technical language with simple business language everywhere\.

Examples:

*[Table view](https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVGoaDz9E=/?moveToWidget=3458764676091713552&cot=14)*

| Avoid | Use Instead |
| --- | --- |
| Analytics | Results |
| Attribution | Customers |
| Referral Tracking | Customer Visits |
| Campaign Performance | Promotion Results |
| Conversion Rate | Interested Customers |
| Revenue Attribution | Revenue Generated |
| Sponsored Placements | Boost Visibility |
