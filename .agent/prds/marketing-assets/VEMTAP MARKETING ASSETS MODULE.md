### Version 1.0

# PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Marketing Assets Module is a self-service tool inside Vemtap that allows businesses to instantly create professional QR marketing materials directly from their dashboard.

The purpose of this feature is to eliminate the need for:

- Graphic designers
- Back-and-forth approvals
- Manual design requests
- Waiting for Vemtap staff to create marketing materials

Instead, businesses can generate ready-to-print marketing assets themselves within minutes.

# PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING

Today, when a business wants a QR poster, table stand, wall design, or promotional material:

1. Business contacts Vemtap
2. Business explains what they need
3. Vemtap collects information
4. Designer creates artwork
5. Business requests changes
6. Design gets approved
7. File is sent for printing

This process:

- Wastes time
- Costs money
- Creates delays
- Does not scale

As Vemtap grows, it becomes impossible for the team to manually design marketing materials for every business.

# SOLUTION

Create a Marketing Assets system that automatically generates professional marketing materials using:

- Business Name
- Business Logo
- Business Brand Colors
- Dynamic QR Code
- Branch Information
- Vemtap Data

Businesses can then:

- Generate assets
- Preview assets
- Download assets
- Print assets

Without contacting Vemtap\.
