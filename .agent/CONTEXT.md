# Project Context: Vemtap

Vemtap is a comprehensive business management and customer engagement platform. It empowers business owners to manage their operations, engage with customers via loyalty programs, and grow through a dedicated affiliate/agent network.

## Core Business Domain

1.  **Business Operations**: Tools for managing businesses, branches, staff, and organizational hierarchies.
2.  **Customer Engagement**: Loyalty programs, omni-channel messaging (SMS, WhatsApp, Internal), surveys, and visitor tracking.
3.  **Product & Sales**: Digital catalogues, product management, cart, and ordering workflows.
4.  **Growth Engine**: An affiliate system for agent-driven growth and the **QR Thrive** module for lead generation.
5.  **Control Tower**: Centralized platform administration, KYC verification, analytics, and impersonation-based support.

## Key Stakeholders

-   **Business Owners**: Manage their business, billing, and high-level reports.
-   **Managers & Staff**: Handle daily branch operations, customer interactions, and order fulfillment.
-   **Customers**: Interact with businesses via QR codes, participate in loyalty programs, and receive targeted messaging.
-   **Agents (Affiliates)**: Partners who refer new businesses to the platform.
-   **Platform Admins**: Operators who manage the ecosystem, verify KYC, and provide support via AMIS (SUDO mode).

## Unique Value Proposition

-   **Seamless Impersonation (AMIS)**: High-level support via context-aware impersonation of branches.
-   **Integrated Loyalty & CRM**: A unified view of customer interactions across multiple channels.
-   **Scalable Monorepo**: Shared logic and types between the NestJS backend and Next.js frontend.
