# VEMTAP Homepage Frontend Redesign — Complete Developer Brief

## 1. Project Goal

We are redesigning the **VEMTAP homepage** to become the main entry point into the VEMTAP discovery platform.

The homepage should no longer feel like a traditional business software/company website.

It should feel like a **modern consumer marketplace and discovery platform**, while still giving businesses a clear and important path into VEMTAP.

The experience should have the energy of platforms such as **Jumia, Konga and other modern marketplace apps**, while maintaining VEMTAP's own identity.

The key feeling should be:

> **“There is something interesting around me. Let me discover it.”**

The user should immediately understand what VEMTAP is and be able to start discovering something without reading a long explanation.

---

# 2. Main Product Idea

VEMTAP connects:

**Consumers ↔ Businesses ↔ Products ↔ Deals**

Consumers come to VEMTAP to:

* Discover businesses.
* Find deals.
* Find products.
* Find services.
* Explore what is around them.
* Search for a particular business, product, deal or location.

Businesses use VEMTAP to:

* Get discovered.
* Promote products and offers.
* Reach nearby consumers.
* Connect with customers.
* Build customer relationships.
* Eventually use VEMTAP's business tools.

The homepage should make the **consumer discovery experience the primary experience**, while keeping businesses clearly included.

---

# 3. Very Important: Mobile First

This is one of the most important requirements.

### Design the mobile experience FIRST.

Do not design desktop first and then squeeze everything into mobile.

The primary design target is a smartphone because VEMTAP is being prepared for:

* Android / Play Store
* iOS / App Store
* Mobile web

The homepage should feel natural enough that someone could believe:

> **“This is the homepage of a mobile app.”**

Desktop and tablet versions must still work properly, but they are adaptations of the mobile design.

### Primary priority

**Mobile → Tablet → Desktop**

Not:

**Desktop → Tablet → Mobile**

**Deal badge appears**

**Location indicator appears**

→

### Preferred animation types

* Gentle floating

* Horizontal scrolling

* Skeleton loading

Avoid excessive animation.

The user should still be able to focus on the content.

# 35. Location Unavailable State

---

# 36. Error States

If API content fails:

> **Something went wrong**

**Try Again**

Do not allow the entire homepage to collapse because one section failed.

Each dynamic section should fail gracefully.

For example, if Featured Deals fails, Nearby Businesses should still load.

---

# 37. Performance

This is very important because the goal is mobile users.

Do not create a visually beautiful homepage that loads slowly.

Prioritize:

* Fast initial load
* Optimized images
* Lazy loading below-the-fold content
* Lightweight animations
* Avoid unnecessary JavaScript
* Avoid huge video backgrounds
* Avoid oversized image assets
* Skeleton loading
* Proper caching
* Responsive images

Animations must not damage performance.

The experience should feel fast even on average mobile devices and weaker connections.

---

# 50. Don't Overdesign

Even though we want something beautiful and catchy, simplicity is still important.

Every design element must have a purpose.

Avoid:

* Too many colors
* Too many fonts
* Too many animations
* Huge paragraphs
* Giant decorative illustrations that do nothing
* Too many CTA buttons
* Excessive gradients
* Complex navigation
* Overloaded cards

The goal is:

> **Simple enough to understand immediately, attractive enough to make people want to explore.**

---

# 56. The Core Message for the Developer

The most important thing to understand is this:

> **We are not simply redesigning the VEMTAP landing page. We are changing the role of the homepage.**
>
> The old homepage mainly explains VEMTAP as a business/customer-management product.
>
> The new homepage should make VEMTAP feel like a **living discovery marketplace** where people can immediately find businesses, deals, products and services around them.
>
> Businesses remain a major part of the ecosystem. The homepage should clearly show that businesses can get discovered and grow through VEMTAP, but the main consumer discovery experience should remain the first thing people see.
>
> The design must be **mobile-first, app-like, clean, modern, animated, attractive and fast**, with the visual energy of a strong marketplace such as Jumia or Konga while maintaining a unique VEMTAP identity.
>
> **The first screen should make someone want to explore VEMTAP.**

---

## One final design direction I strongly recommend

For the hero animation, don't settle for generic floating shapes.

**Design an actual VEMTAP visual world.**

Imagine a user's location in the middle, with beautifully designed mini cards representing:

**Restaurant → Deal → Fashion Store → Product → Beauty Business → Special Offer → Nearby Business**

They gently appear, move and reorganize around the location.

Then the hero message says:

# **Discover What's Near You**

That one visual can become one of the strongest pieces of VEMTAP's identity. It tells the story of the product **without needing a paragraph to explain it**.

The developer should treat that hero visual as a real product-design task, not merely decoration.
