# STITCH AI PROMPT

# SECTION 7: CUSTOMER ACQUISITION

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Customer Acquisition experience for Vemtap\.

This is the customer\-facing experience that occurs after a customer scans a Vemtap QR Code\.

This is one of the most critical parts of the platform because every additional field, click, or confusion can reduce customer registrations\.

The experience must be:

- Extremely simple
- Fast
- Mobile\-first
- Trustworthy
- Friendly
- Conversion optimized

The customer should be able to complete the entire process in less than 30 seconds\.

The primary goal is:

**Convert a visitor into a captured customer lead\.**
