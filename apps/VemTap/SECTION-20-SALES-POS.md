# SECTION 20: SALES &amp; POS

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Sales &amp; POS \(Point of Sale\) module for Vemtap\.

This is one of the most important and frequently used modules in the entire platform\.

Business owners and staff will use this module every day to process customer purchases, record transactions, manage sales, attach customers to purchases, generate receipts, and track business performance\.

Unlike traditional POS systems, Vemtap POS is deeply connected to:

- Customer CRM
- Customer Profiles
- Customer Spending History
- Orders
- Products &amp; Services
- Analytics
- Loyalty Programs \(Future\)
- Customer Lifetime Value
- Discovery Network

### Key Differentiator

Every sale can be attached to a customer profile\.

This means Vemtap automatically builds:

- Purchase History
- Spending History
- Visit History
- Customer Value
- Customer Insights

The POS should feel:

- Extremely fast
- Easy for cashiers
- Easy for business owners
- Mobile\-first
- Touch\-friendly
- Retail\-ready
