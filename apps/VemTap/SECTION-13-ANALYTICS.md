# SECTION 13: ANALYTICS

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Analytics module for Vemtap\.

The Analytics module is where businesses understand the performance of everything happening inside Vemtap\.

This module should transform raw data into easy\-to\-understand business insights\.

Business owners should be able to answer questions like:

- How many customers have I captured?
- Is my customer database growing?
- Are customers engaging with my campaigns?
- Which QR codes perform best?
- Which locations generate the most scans?
- How many customers return?
- Which campaigns generate results?
- How is my business growing over time?

The experience should be designed for non\-technical business owners\.

Focus on:

- Simplicity
- Clarity
- Actionable insights
- Visual storytelling
- Business growth tracking
