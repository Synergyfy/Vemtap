# SECTION 9: MESSAGING CENTER

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Messaging Center for Vemtap\.

The Messaging Center is where businesses communicate with customers captured through:

- QR Code Registrations
- NFC Taps
- Customer Forms
- Orders
- Customer Segments
- Discovery Network Activities

This is one of the highest\-value areas of Vemtap because it helps businesses turn captured customer data into repeat visits, sales, loyalty, and long\-term relationships\.

The experience must be simple enough for a small business owner while powerful enough for larger businesses\.

The primary goal is:

**Help businesses easily send targeted messages to the right customers at the right time\.**
