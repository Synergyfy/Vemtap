# Vemtap Customer Experience Flow Restructure

## Goal

We already have the pages and features.

What we need now is to rearrange them in a cleaner and simpler way so business owners can easily understand:

- Their customer experience
- Their business link
- Their QR code
- How customers will see everything

The main idea is:

The business owner should feel like they are building ONE customer experience.

Not managing separate pages.

The QR code and business link should feel like part of the same experience.

---

# STEP-BY-STEP FLOW FOR FRONTEND REARRANGEMENT

# STEP 1 — CREATE ONE MAIN PAGE CALLED:

# “Customer Experience”

Instead of separating:

- User Experience Page
- Unique Business Link Page
- QR Code Page

Everything should now be connected inside ONE main page.

This page becomes the main place where businesses manage how customers will interact with them.

---

# STEP 2 — TOP SECTION OF THE PAGE

At the top of the page, show:

## Business Link

Example:

vemtap.com/store/businessname

---

## QR Code

Place the QR code beside the link.

---

## Buttons

Add:

- Copy Link
- Download QR
- Preview Experience
- Open on Phone

This helps businesses instantly understand:

“This is what my customers will scan or open.”

---

# STEP 3 — SPLIT THE PAGE INTO TWO SECTIONS

The page should now have:

## LEFT SIDE

Settings and arrangement area.

## RIGHT SIDE

Live mobile preview.

The right side should always show how customers will see the experience.

As businesses make changes on the left side, the preview updates instantly.

---

# STEP 4 — LEFT SIDE SETTINGS AREA

Create sections for:

- Visitor Form
- Products
- Services
- Forms
- WhatsApp Button
- Website Link
- Social Links
- Booking
- PDF/Menu
- Loyalty
- Coupons
- Other Features

Each section should have:

- Edit button
- Enable/Disable toggle
- Drag and move option

This allows businesses to rearrange the order customers will see.

Example:

1. Products
2. WhatsApp Chat
3. Booking
4. Feedback Form

or

1. Booking
2. Products
3. Loyalty
4. WhatsApp

The arrangement should reflect immediately on the mobile preview.

---

# STEP 5 — VISITOR FORM SETTINGS

Inside the Visitor Form section:

Allow businesses to:

- Turn form ON or OFF
- Edit welcome text
- Edit success message
- Choose if customers must fill form first
- Choose if customers can skip form

---

## Form Fields

Default fields:

- Name
- Phone Number
- Email

Optional:

Businesses can add more fields.

---

## Success Message

After customer submits form:

Example:

“Thank you for visiting us.”

“Enjoy browsing our products.”

Businesses should be able to customize this.

---

# STEP 6 — LIVE MOBILE PREVIEW

The right side of the page should behave like a real phone preview.

This is very important.

When businesses click Preview Experience or when they edit settings, the preview should simulate the real customer flow.

---

## Preview Flow Should Show:

### 1. Opening Screen

Optional logo and welcome message.

---

### 2. Visitor Form Popup

Exactly how customers will see it.

---

### 3. Success Message

After form submission.

---

### 4. Main Experience

Products, services, buttons, booking, WhatsApp, forms, etc.

All based on the arrangement chosen by the business.

---

# STEP 7 — DRAG AND DROP ARRANGEMENT

Businesses should be able to drag sections up and down.

Example:

Move:

- WhatsApp above Products
- Booking above Services
- Loyalty below Forms

The mobile preview should rearrange instantly.

This gives businesses control over their customer experience.

---

# STEP 8 — PUBLISH FLOW

At the top or bottom of the page, add:

- Save Draft
- Preview
- Publish

---

## Save Draft

Saves changes without affecting live customer experience.

---

## Preview

Shows full customer flow before publishing.

---

## Publish

Makes the changes live instantly.

The business link and QR code should automatically use the latest published version.

---

# STEP 9 — OPEN ON PHONE FEATURE

Add button:

# “Open on My Phone”

When clicked:

- Show QR code for testing
OR
- Generate temporary preview link

This helps businesses test the experience directly on mobile.

Very important because most businesses will use phones.

---

# STEP 10 — REMOVE CONFUSION IN THE DASHBOARD

Do not make businesses feel like they are managing:

- QR code separately
- Link separately
- User experience separately

Everything should now feel like ONE connected experience.

The flow should feel like:

# “Build Your Customer Experience”

Then automatically:

- Vemtap generates the link
- Vemtap generates the QR code
- Customers see the experience

Simple.

---

# FINAL USER FLOW

# BUSINESS OWNER FLOW

1. Login
2. Open Customer Experience page
3. Add products/services/forms/buttons
4. Rearrange sections
5. Customize visitor form
6. Preview mobile experience
7. Publish
8. Share QR code or business link

---

# CUSTOMER FLOW

1. Scan QR code or open business link
2. See welcome screen
3. Fill visitor form
4. See success message
5. Access products/services/forms/WhatsApp/booking/etc.

---

# IMPORTANT DESIGN DIRECTION

The experience should feel:

- Simple
- Mobile-first
- Easy to understand
- Visual
- Fast
- Clean

The business owner should immediately understand:

“This is exactly how my customers will experience my business.”
