# SECTION 8: CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT \(CRM\)

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Customer Management \(CRM\) module for Vemtap\.

This section is where businesses manage all customers captured through:

- QR Code Scans
- NFC Taps
- Customer Registration Forms
- Orders
- Campaign Responses
- Future Integrations

The CRM should feel like the central intelligence hub of the business\.

Business owners should easily:

- View customers
- Search customers
- Track customer history
- Monitor engagement
- Segment audiences
- Export data
- Understand customer behavior

The design should be simple enough for small businesses while powerful enough for growing businesses\.
