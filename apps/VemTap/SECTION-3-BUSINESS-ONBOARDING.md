# SECTION 3: BUSINESS ONBOARDING

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Business Onboarding experience for Vemtap\.

This section begins immediately after a business successfully creates and verifies their account\.

The goal is to help the business:

- Complete their profile
- Configure their business information
- Select a subscription plan
- Make payment if required
- Become ready to start capturing customers

The onboarding should feel:

- Simple
- Guided
- Professional
- Friendly
- Fast
- Non\-technical

Business owners should always know:

- Where they are
- What step they are on
- What happens next
