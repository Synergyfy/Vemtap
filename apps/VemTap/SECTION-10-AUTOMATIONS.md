# SECTION 10: AUTOMATIONS

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Automation Center for Vemtap\.

The Automation Center allows businesses to automatically communicate with customers without manually creating campaigns every time\.

The purpose of this module is to help businesses:

- Save time
- Increase customer engagement
- Improve retention
- Encourage repeat visits
- Build customer loyalty
- Reactivate inactive customers

Automations should be simple enough for a small business owner to understand while powerful enough for advanced marketing workflows\.

The experience should feel:

- Smart
- Automated
- Easy to configure
- Easy to monitor
- Easy to edit

The business should be able to activate automations within minutes\.
