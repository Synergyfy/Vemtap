> **VEMTAP helps people discover what to buy, where to go, and what deals are available around them — while helping businesses get discovered and build relationships with customers.**

The homepage should therefore feel like a **discovery marketplace first**, with the business platform sitting behind it.

# Proposed VEMTAP Homepage

## 1. Header / Navigation

Keep this extremely simple.

**VEMTAP logo**

**Discover** | **Deals** | **Categories** | **Businesses**

Right side:

**For Businesses**
**Log In**

On mobile, I would keep the header even simpler:

**VEMTAP logo**                 ☰

But the menu should still expose:

* Discover
* Deals
* Businesses
* Categories
* For Businesses
* Login

I would not make the consumer dig through a hamburger just to find deals. On desktop, **Deals** should be visible.

---

# 2. Hero: Don't Talk About VEMTAP — Give Me Something to Do

This is the biggest change.

### Current type of hero

> Turn every customer into a customer you can reach again.

That is good business software messaging, but it doesn't answer the question a consumer has when they arrive.

Instead:

# **Discover What's Near You**

### Find amazing deals, businesses, products and services around you.

Then immediately give them two actions:

### **📍 Find Deals Near Me**

and underneath:

**🔍 Search deals, businesses, products or locations**

For example, the search placeholder could say:

> *Try “restaurants”, “shoes”, “Apo”, or “Samsung”*

Then perhaps underneath:

**Popular near you:**
Food · Fashion · Beauty · Electronics · Services

This makes the website useful within seconds.

---

# 3. Don't Force Location Immediately

This is important.

I would **not** immediately throw a browser location permission popup at everybody who visits.

Some people will deny it.

Instead:

**📍 Find Deals Near Me**

When they click it:

> **Allow VEMTAP to use your location**
>
> We'll use your location to show relevant businesses and deals around you.

Then request permission.

And for people who don't want to share:

> **Search a location instead**

So the user has control.

---

# 4. Featured Deals Should Be Immediately Visible

This is where your consumer marketplace starts to feel real.

Under the hero:

# 🔥 Featured Deals

**Deals people are checking out right now**

Then 3–6 deal cards.

Each card should have:

**Business image**

**20% OFF**

Business name

Category

Location

Short deal description

Original price → deal price, where applicable

**View Deal**

For example:

> **20% OFF**
>
> Bella Restaurant
> 🍔 Food & Drinks
> Wuse 2, Abuja
>
> **₦12,000 → ₦9,600**
>
> **View Deal →**

This section is critical because somebody who doesn't know what VEMTAP is should immediately think:

> **“Oh, this is where I can find deals.”**

---

# 5. "Around You" Section

After featured deals:

# 📍 Popular Around You

Once location is known:

> Deals and businesses around **[location]**

Then a horizontal/scrollable set of cards.

### Deals

### Businesses

### Products

You could eventually have tabs:

**Deals | Businesses | Products**

That allows VEMTAP to grow beyond coupons.

A consumer might not be looking for a discount. They might simply be looking for:

> “Phone shop near me”

or

> “Tailor near me”

or

> “Restaurant around me.”

So the broader promise should remain **discovery**, not simply discounting.

# 7. Featured Businesses

This is where you start showing businesses even when they don't have a deal.

# 🏪 Discover Businesses

> Find businesses, products and services around you.

Cards could show:

**Business image**

**Business name**

Category

Rating/reviews later

Location

Opening status later

**View Business →**

This is strategically important.

Because you don't want consumers to develop the mental model:

> **VEMTAP = discount website.**

You want:

> **VEMTAP = place where I discover businesses and opportunities around me.**

Deals are the **hook**.

Discovery is the **platform**.

---

# 8. A Strong "Why VEMTAP?" Consumer Section

You don't need a giant corporate explanation.

Just answer:

### **Why people use VEMTAP**

**Discover more**
Find businesses and products you may not have known existed.

**Find better deals**
Discover offers and promotions around you.

**Explore locally**
See what's available in your area or another location.

**Connect directly**
Call, WhatsApp, visit or interact with businesses.

This section is about **consumer value**, not explaining the technology.

Don't waste homepage space explaining NFC, CRM, analytics, customer capture, etc.

Those belong to the business side.

---

# 9. Then Introduce the Business Side

This is where we transition.

Instead of abruptly switching from consumer marketplace to:

> “We help businesses capture customer data…”

make it feel like a natural second audience.

For example:

---

# **Own a Business? Get Discovered on VEMTAP.**

Put your business in front of people actively looking for products, services, businesses and deals around them.

### Reach more people

Get discovered by consumers searching around your location.

### Showcase your offers

Promote deals, products, services and special offers.

### Build customer relationships

Capture and engage customers beyond the first visit.

### Understand your customers

Use insights and analytics to understand visits and engagement.

**For Businesses →**

That button should take them to the business landing page.

---

# 10. Introduce the VEMTAP QR Network Here

I wouldn't make the homepage hero about QR codes.

But I **would absolutely explain the network somewhere on the homepage**, because this is one of your differentiators.

Something like:

# **See VEMTAP Around Your City**

> Look out for VEMTAP QR codes at businesses and locations around you.

**Scan → Discover → Explore → Save**

You can show a simple graphic of:

**VEMTAP QR**

↓

**Local Discovery**

↓

**Deals + Businesses**

This helps consumers understand why they may start seeing VEMTAP physically around their city.

And importantly, this section is also powerful for businesses because they can understand:

> “Oh, VEMTAP isn't just a website. They're building a physical discovery network.”

---

↓

↓

Don't turn this into a five-section software explanation.

# But I would make one important architectural change

I would **not make all of those sections static**.

The homepage should become increasingly personalized.

For someone in Abuja:

> **Deals in Abuja**

For someone in Lagos:

> **Deals in Lagos**

For someone who has selected Wuse:

> **Deals around Wuse**

For someone who hasn't given location:

> **Popular Deals on VEMTAP**

So the homepage effectively has two states.

### State 1 — Location unknown

> **Discover What's Near You**
>
> Use my location / Search location
>
> Popular Deals
>
> Popular Businesses
>
> Categories

### State 2 — Location known

> **Discover What's Near You**
>
> 📍 Apo, Abuja
>
> **Deals Near You**
>
> **Businesses Near You**
>
> **Popular in Apo**
>
> etc.

That would make the site feel much more like a **real discovery platform** rather than a static marketing website.

---

# And there is a deeper strategic principle here

I would divide everything VEMTAP does into three layers:

### **DISCOVER**

Consumer-facing.

> Deals
> Businesses
> Products
> Services
> Locations

### **ENGAGE**

The interaction layer.

> Chat
> WhatsApp
> Offers
> Loyalty
> Reviews
> Referrals

### **GROW**

The business layer.

> Customer capture
> CRM
> Analytics
> Messaging
> Marketing
> Retention

The consumer primarily experiences:

**DISCOVER → ENGAGE**

The business experiences:

**DISCOVER → ENGAGE → GROW**

And **that is a much clearer story for VEMTAP than trying to put every feature on the homepage.**

---

## One thing I would change from the current website immediately

Do **not** try to preserve every existing homepage section just because it already exists.

Your current business material about customer capture, retention, analytics, NFC, etc. can remain valuable, but the homepage no longer needs to carry all of it.

The homepage has one primary job:

> **Get a person who doesn't know VEMTAP to discover something useful within seconds.**

Then a secondary job:

> **Make a business owner realize that VEMTAP is where their customers can discover them.**

That creates a very clean relationship:

**Consumer:**
“Let me see what's available around me.”

**Business:**
“I want my business to be part of that.”

That is the homepage I would build toward.
