This is the complete screen inventory for the Discovery Network Super Admin module\.

The goal is to ensure developers and designers do not miss any screen, workflow, modal, table, detail page, analytics page, approval page, settings page, or management page\.

- Left Sidebar Navigation

- Tables, Filters, Analytics Cards, Charts
- Bulk Actions Supported
- Search Everywhere
- Export Functionality
- Pagination
- Advanced Filtering
