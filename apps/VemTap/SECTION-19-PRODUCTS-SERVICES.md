# SECTION 19: PRODUCTS &amp; SERVICES

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Products &amp; Services module for Vemtap\.

This module is one of the most important foundations of the platform\.

Before a business can:

- Sell products
- Receive orders
- Track inventory
- Use POS
- Generate sales analytics
- Track customer spending
- Build purchase history

Vemtap must first know what products and services the business offers\.

This module serves as the foundation for:

### Current Features

- Orders &amp; Bookings
- Customer Profiles
- Customer Purchase History
- Business Catalogs
- QR Menus
- Service Catalogs

### Future Features

- POS System
- Inventory Management
- Revenue Analytics
- Sales Reports
- Product Performance Analytics
- Customer Lifetime Value
- Multi\-Location Inventory

The system should support:

### Product\-Based Businesses

- Restaurants
- Cafes
- Bakeries
- Fashion Stores
- Supermarkets
- Electronics Stores
- Pharmacies \(Future Enterprise\)
- Retail Stores

### Service\-Based Businesses

- Salons
- Barbershops
- Gyms
- Spas
- Car Washes
- Repair Services
- Consultants
- Professional Services

The experience should feel:

- Simple
- Fast
- Organized
- Inventory\-ready
- POS\-ready
- Mobile\-first
