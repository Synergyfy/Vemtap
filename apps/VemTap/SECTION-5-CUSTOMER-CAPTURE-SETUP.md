# SECTION 5: CUSTOMER CAPTURE SETUP

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Customer Capture Setup experience for Vemtap\.

This section is one of the most important parts of the platform because it creates the mechanism businesses use to capture customer information\.

The objective is simple:

A business should be able to:

1. Generate a QR Code
2. Create a Short Link
3. Customize the QR Code
4. Configure the Customer Capture Form
5. Preview the Customer Experience
6. Download and Deploy the QR Code

At the end of this section, the business should be fully ready to start collecting customer data\.

The experience should feel:

- Fast
- Exciting
- Professional
- Simple
- Mobile\-first
- Non\-technical
