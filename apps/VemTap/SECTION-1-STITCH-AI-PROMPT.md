# SECTION 1 STITCH AI PROMPT

## PROJECT INFORMATION

Design a complete mobile\-first responsive website for a SaaS platform called **Vemtap**\.

Vemtap helps businesses capture customer information using QR codes and NFC technology, build customer databases, send smart marketing messages, increase repeat visits, and grow their business\.

The website should feel:

- Modern
- Premium
- Clean
- Trustworthy
- Easy to understand
- Conversion\-focused

Target audience:

- Restaurants
- Fashion Stores
- Salons
- Gyms
- Supermarkets
- Hotels
- Barbershops
- Small Businesses
- Medium Businesses
