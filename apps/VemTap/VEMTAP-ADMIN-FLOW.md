My recommendation is to simplify even further for Version 1:

### Admin only needs to setup:

1. Asset Types
2. Templates
3. QR Destinations
4. CTA Library
5. Placement Guides

Everything else \(analytics, recommendations, business monitoring\) can largely be automated by Vemtap and exposed as reporting screens\.

That gives you a much faster path to launch while still allowing businesses to create Posters, Flyers, Table Tents, Counter Displays, Stickers, Roll\-Up Banners, Business Cards, and Social Media Graphics without any missing functionality\.
