# SECTION 16: REFERRALS &amp; COMMISSIONS

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Referrals &amp; Commissions module for Vemtap\.

This module transforms businesses from platform users into growth partners\.

The objective is to allow businesses to:

- Refer other businesses
- Earn commissions
- Track referrals
- Monitor subscription revenue generated
- View commission earnings
- Request payouts
- Build recurring income through Vemtap referrals

This system supports Vemtap's partner\-driven growth strategy\.

### Referral Model

Business A refers Business B\.

Business B subscribes to Vemtap\.

Business A earns commission\.

Commissions continue based on Vemtap's referral rules\.

The system should feel:

- Transparent
- Motivating
- Easy to understand
- Trustworthy
- Revenue\-focused

Businesses should clearly see:

- How much they have earned
- Who they referred
- What commissions are pending
- What commissions have been paid
