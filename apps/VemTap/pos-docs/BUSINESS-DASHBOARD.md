# BUSINESS DASHBOARD

# POS &amp; INVENTORY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

# 1\. DASHBOARD

The business overview screen\.

Provides quick visibility into:

- Today's Sales
- Today's Transactions
- Today's Orders
- Products Sold
- Revenue
- Inventory Value
- Low Stock Products
- Out of Stock Products
- Recent Transactions
- Recent Inventory Activities

- Held Sales
