# What Is Vemtap POS?

Vemtap POS is the system your staff uses when selling products or services to customers\.

Instead of writing sales in a notebook or trying to remember what was sold, everything is recorded automatically\.

Think of it as:

**Your Cashier \+ Inventory Manager \+ Customer Database \+ Receipt Book \+ Marketing Assistant all inside one system\.**

# What Is Inventory?

Inventory simply means:

Everything you have available to sell\.

For example:

A supermarket may have:

- 100 bottles of Coke
- 50 loaves of bread
- 200 packs of Indomie

A fashion store may have:

- 20 Blue Shirts
- 15 Black Trousers
- 30 Belts

A pharmacy may have:

- 100 Paracetamol
- 50 Vitamin C
- 20 Malaria Drugs

Inventory helps you know:

- What you have
- What is running out
- What needs to be reordered
