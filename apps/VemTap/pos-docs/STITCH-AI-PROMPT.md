## Design Requirements

Design a modern, clean, mobile\-first POS &amp; Inventory Management application for business owners, store managers, cashiers, and inventory staff\.

Design style:

- Mobile\-first
- Simple and easy to understand
- Fast cashier workflow
- Bottom navigation where appropriate
- Card\-based layout
- Modern SaaS design
- Clean spacing
- Minimal dropdowns
- Use buttons and cards instead
- Optimized for one\-hand usage
- Professional retail POS appearance
