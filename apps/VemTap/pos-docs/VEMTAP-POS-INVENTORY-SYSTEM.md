# ROLE\-BASED STAFF ACCESS

### Cashier

Can Access:

- Dashboard
- POS
- Customers
- Orders
- Sales
- Receipts
- Payments
- Register
- Notifications
- Profile

Cannot Access:

- Staff Management
- Roles &amp; Permissions
- Tax Settings
- Business Settings

### Inventory Officer

Can Access:

- Inventory
- Stock Receiving
- Stock Adjustment
- Stock Transfers
- Stock Counting
- Products
- Suppliers
- Notifications
- Tasks

### Store Manager

Can Access:

- POS
- Orders
- Customers
- Inventory
- Products
- Staff Activities
- Reports \(if permitted\)
- Register

### Branch Manager

Can Access:

- All branch operations
- Staff supervision
- Inventory supervision
- Sales supervision
- Reports \(branch only\)
