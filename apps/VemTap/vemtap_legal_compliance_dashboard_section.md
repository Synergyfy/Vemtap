# VEMTAP LEGAL & COMPLIANCE DASHBOARD SECTION

## 🎯 Purpose

The Legal & Compliance section is designed to:

- Provide transparency to businesses
- Give easy access to all legal documents
- Build trust with users and enterprise clients
- Ensure compliance with data protection laws

---

## 🧭 Navigation Placement

Add a new menu item in the dashboard sidebar:

**Menu Name:** Legal & Compliance

Position:
- Below Settings
- Or inside Settings as a dedicated tab

---

## 🏠 Main Overview Screen

### Section Title:
**Legal & Compliance**

### Description:
Access all legal documents, agreements, and compliance information related to your use of Vemtap.
