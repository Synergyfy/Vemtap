# SECTION 4: FIRST\-TIME DASHBOARD EXPERIENCE

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first First\-Time Dashboard Experience for Vemtap\.

This section begins immediately after a business successfully completes onboarding\.

This is one of the most important parts of the platform because many businesses sign up for software but never fully activate their account\.

The purpose of this section is to guide the business to their first success as quickly as possible\.

The business should never feel lost\.

The dashboard should clearly tell them:

- What to do next
- What is incomplete
- What actions generate results
- How close they are to becoming fully activated

The primary objective is:

**Help the business capture their first customer as fast as possible\.**
