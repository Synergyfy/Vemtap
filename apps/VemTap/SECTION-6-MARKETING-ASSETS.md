# SECTION 6: MARKETING ASSETS

## Goal

Help businesses create, customize, preview, and download professional QR\-powered marketing materials for both physical and digital use\.

The experience should be mobile\-first, simple, guided, and easy for non\-technical business owners\.

Do not create a complex Canva\-style editor\.

Use a step\-by\-step customization flow similar to personalized product experiences on e\-commerce platforms like Temu\.

All templates should automatically pre\-fill available business information:

- Business Name
- Logo
- Brand Colors
- QR Code
- Website
- Phone Number
- Social Links

Users can edit these fields before downloading\.

### Stitch Design Instructions

- Mobile\-first design
- Simple onboarding flow
- One primary action per screen
- Large touch\-friendly buttons
- No dropdown\-heavy interfaces
- Live preview throughout customization process
- Professional SaaS dashboard styling
- Consistent with Vemtap Business Dashboard design system
- Focus on speed, simplicity, and ease of use for small business owners with little or no design experience

# This structure is much closer to a production\-ready Vemtap Marketing Assets system than the original 7\-screen version because it covers the complete asset creation journey from template selection to customization, preview, download, storage, and future analytics\.
