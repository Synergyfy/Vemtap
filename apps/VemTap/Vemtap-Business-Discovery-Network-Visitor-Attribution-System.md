Let's use a simple example\.

Imagine there are 100 people walking around Wuse 2 today\.

Some are:

The problem is:

Most businesses don't know these people exist\.

A restaurant doesn't know who entered the fashion store\.

A barbershop doesn't know who entered the supermarket\.

A pharmacy doesn't know who entered the restaurant\.

Even though they are all close to each other\.

Because of this, businesses spend money on:

- Facebook Ads
- Instagram Ads

- Flyers
- Billboards

Hoping people will find them\.

Most times, these advertisements are shown to people who may never visit\.

# Vemtap's New Idea

Instead of advertising to random people online,

This is much more valuable\.

Why?

Because these people are already outside\.

They are already spending money\.

They are already visiting businesses\.

They are already potential customers\.

# Anti\-Spam Protection

One danger is overwhelming customers\.

Example:

Customer enters one store\.

Receives:

- 15 SMS
- 20 Emails
- 30 Notifications

Customers will become annoyed\.

To prevent this:

Vemtap applies rules\.

Example:

```
Maximum 3 offers per visit

Maximum 1 offer per business

Maximum 1 notification every 24 hours
```

This keeps the experience useful\.

# Customer Privacy

Customers must always control what they receive\.

Settings should allow:

```
Receive Promotions

Yes / No

Receive SMS

Yes / No

Receive Emails

Yes / No

Receive Push Notifications

Yes / No
```

Without consent, no promotional communication should be sent\.
