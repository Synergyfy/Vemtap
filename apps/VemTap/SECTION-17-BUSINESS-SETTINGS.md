# SECTION 17: BUSINESS SETTINGS

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Business Settings module for Vemtap\.

This module gives business owners complete control over their account, business profile, team, subscription, billing, notifications, security, and future integrations\.

The Settings module should feel:

- Clean
- Organized
- Secure
- Professional
- Easy to navigate

Business owners should always know:

- Who has access to the account
- What plan they are on
- What they are paying for
- How notifications are configured
- How their account is secured

This module acts as the control center of the entire Vemtap platform\.
