# SECTION 15: DISCOVERY NETWORK

## PROJECT CONTEXT

Design the complete mobile\-first Discovery Network module for Vemtap\.

This is one of the most strategic and powerful parts of the Vemtap ecosystem\.

This is where Vemtap evolves from being only a customer capture platform into a business discovery and customer acquisition network\.

The Discovery Network allows businesses to become discoverable by customers who have already entered the Vemtap ecosystem through another business\.

### Example

A customer scans a QR code at:

- Restaurant A

The customer joins Vemtap\.

Now the customer can discover:

- Nearby Restaurants
- Nearby Fashion Stores
- Nearby Salons
- Nearby Gyms
- Nearby Hotels
- Nearby Electronics Stores
- Nearby Service Providers

Businesses can promote offers and campaigns inside the network\.

### Example Promotions

Buy One Get One Free

20% Discount

Free Consultation

Happy Hour

Weekend Specials

Limited\-Time Promotions

The Discovery Network creates an additional traffic channel for businesses\.

The primary goals are:

- Help businesses get discovered
- Generate customer traffic
- Increase customer acquisition
- Increase customer engagement
- Increase business visibility
- Create network effects inside Vemtap

This module should feel like:

- A business growth engine
- A marketplace
- A discovery platform
- A customer acquisition network
